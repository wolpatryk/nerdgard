Nerdgard is a private friends-only Discord server with a website at nerdgard.pl. Not public — no invite links or recruitment.

The community is into Pepe/frog culture. The logo (green frog face emblem) is their herb/coat of arms and is treated seriously.

**Why:** The site is an internal hub for the friend group, not a public-facing community page.

**How to apply:** Design should respect the emblem — treat it like a real crest/coat of arms, not a meme. Frog/Pepe aesthetic is welcome but the logo itself is serious. No public Discord invite buttons or open recruitment features.
